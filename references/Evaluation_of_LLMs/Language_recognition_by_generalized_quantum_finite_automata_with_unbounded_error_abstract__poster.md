Research on theoretical models of practically implementable quantum computers has focused on variants of the quantum finite automaton (QFA). The bounded-error language recognition capabilities of various alternative QFA types have been analyzed and compared with their classical counterparts. In this paper, we examine the computational power of QFA’s in the unbounded error setting. Extending previous work [8], we show that all one-way QFA models that are at least as general as Kondacs-Watrous QFA’s (KWQFA’s) [3] are equivalent in power to classical probabilistic finite automata in this setting. In the bounded-error case, some of these generalized machines recognize more languages than the KWQFA.
